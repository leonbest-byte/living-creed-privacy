Privacy Policy for Living Creed

Effective Date: July 29, 2026
Last Updated: July 29, 2026

Welcome to Living Creed (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data with transparency. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use the Living Creed mobile application (the “App”) and related authentication services.

By using Living Creed, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, secure, and improve our services. Depending on how you interact with Living Creed, we collect:

A. Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Credentials: Email address, password (encrypted), and profile details created during registration.
  • Third-Party Authentication Data: If you sign in using Meta / Facebook Login, Google Sign-In, or Firebase Authentication, we receive profile information authorized by you via the third party (e.g., your name, primary email address, profile picture, and unique user ID).
  • User Content: Preferences, swipe history, interactions, and saved items within the App.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Data: Device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, and IP address.
  • Usage Data: App activity, feature interaction, and crash logs collected for diagnostic purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for the following purposes:

  1. Authentication & Account Management: To create, maintain, and authenticate your user account.
  2. App Functionality: To display personalized content, save user preferences, and execute core app features.
  3. Security & Integrity: To prevent unauthorized access, investigate suspicious activity, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  4. App Optimization: To monitor app performance, fix bugs, and improve user interface design.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party advertisers.

3. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

Living Creed relies on trusted third-party service providers to handle backend operations and authentication:

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): Powers our database, authentication management, cloud functions, and analytics. Firebase processes data under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook Login): Used strictly as an identity provider to authenticate users. Meta's handling of user data is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

4. Data Deletion Instructions (Meta Compliance)

Living Creed provides users with full control over their personal data. In compliance with Meta Developer Policies, you may request the deletion of any data obtained through Facebook Login at any time:

Option A: Direct Account Deletion in App

Navigate to Settings → Account within the Living Creed app and select Delete Account. All profile details, saved states, and linked credentials will be permanently removed.

Option B: Facebook Settings Method

  1. Log into your Facebook Account.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.
  3. Select Living Creed and click Remove.
  4. Submit a Send Request to Living Creed to wipe associated profile data.

Option C: Manual Deletion Request

You can email our team directly at privacy@livingcreedapp.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will complete the deletion within 7 business days.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill operational requirements or satisfy legal obligations. All network requests are encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS (TLS/SSL) protocols during transit.

6. Children's Privacy

Living Creed does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will promptly delete that information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
